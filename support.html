Depometre Support

Real consumption, cost per kilometer and a tank estimate from your fuel receipt

Contact: depometre.destek@gmail.com

When you write, include the app version: Settings → About (for example “1.0 (1)”).

Why is my consumption still not showing?

Depometre does not estimate consumption; it measures it. The measurement is made between two full tanks that both have an odometer reading:

  1. When you fill the tank completely, turn on “Full tank” in the entry and enter the odometer reading.
  2. Do the same at the next full tank.

The first real figure appears after the second full tank. Record the partial fill-ups in between too; they are included in the calculation as well.

If a full-tank entry is missing its odometer reading, you can tap the warning on the Dashboard and add the reading to that entry.

The receipt could not be read, or was read wrong

  • Every field that was read can be corrected on the confirmation screen. Compare it with the receipt before saving.
  • Fields marked in orange are values that could not be read with full certainty. A total marked “calculated” could not be read from the receipt and was calculated from the liters and the unit price.
  • If the receipt cannot be read at all, you can enter the details by hand; if you don't have the receipt, use the “Enter manually” option.
  • For a better result, photograph the receipt on a flat surface, without shadows, with all of it in view.

The tank estimate does not match my car's gauge

The tank and range values do not come from a sensor; they are an estimate based on the fuel and odometer readings you have recorded since your last full tank. Your car's own gauge is always more accurate.

  • The estimate is calculated from the odometer reading you last recorded or entered manually. If you have driven without filling up, enter your current odometer reading with “Update odometer” on the tank card.
  • If you see the “The estimate has gone outside the tank's limits” warning, fill the tank and record it; the estimate resets.
  • The estimate needs the tank capacity to be entered in the vehicle details.

I see the “Your data couldn't be opened” screen

Do not delete the app. Your entries are most likely still on your phone, and deleting the app deletes them too. Close the app completely and open it again. If the problem continues, use the “Share error details” button on that screen to send the text to the address above.

Can you see or recover my data?

No. Your entries and receipt photos stay only on your phone; nothing reaches us. That is why we cannot see your data and cannot recover data that has been lost. If iCloud or computer backup is turned on for your phone, the app's data is included in that backup.

Consumption does not appear for my Gasoline + LPG vehicle

For dual-fuel vehicles, spending tracking works; consumption and the tank estimate are not supported yet.
