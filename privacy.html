Depometre Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0

In short: Depometre collects no data. Your receipts, odometer readings and entries stay on your phone only. The app does not connect to the internet; there are no accounts, ads or analytics.

Data collected

None. Depometre has no accounts and no sign-up. The app contains no analytics, no ads and no third-party software components. The app has no server of its own, and the app sends no requests to the internet.

The “Privacy Policy” and “Support” links in Settings open these pages in Safari; the app itself does not make a connection when they do.

Stored on your phone

The information you enter is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • Vehicles: name, optional plate, fuel type, tank capacity, odometer information.
  • Fuel entries: date, liters, unit price, total, fuel type, odometer reading, whether you filled the tank completely, station, city, payment type and your notes.
  • Receipt photos: the receipt images you take or choose.
  • A single setting: whether you have seen the introduction screen.

Receipt photos can contain anything printed on the receipt, for example the plate, station details or a loyalty card number. These images do not leave your phone.

Reading receipts

The text on a receipt is read on your phone, with Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo is not sent to any server.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only to photograph receipts, and permission is asked the first time it is used.
  • When you choose a receipt from your photo library, no permission is asked: the system's picker gives the app only the photo you choose, and the app cannot see the rest of your library.

Backups

If iCloud or computer backup is turned on for your phone, the app's data is included in that backup. That is the backup Apple provides and you manage; Depometre has no separate backup or server.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete entries and vehicles one by one in the app.
  • When you delete a vehicle, its entries are not deleted; they remain without a vehicle, and you can delete them separately if you want to.
  • To delete everything, removing the app from your phone is enough.

Children

Depometre is not an app directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, and therefore none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that requires exchanging data is added to the app in the future (for example, current fuel prices), this page will be updated before that feature is released, and will state clearly what is sent and why.
